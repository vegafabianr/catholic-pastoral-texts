# Data license

The texts and metadata in this repository are released under [Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/).

Two exceptions keep their own terms:

- `ocr-piloto/modernizacion-lexica.csv` derives from the lexicon of IMPACT-es and is released under CC BY-SA 3.0.
- `ocr-piloto/diccionario-es/` is the Hunspell `es_ES` dictionary, with its license in that folder.
